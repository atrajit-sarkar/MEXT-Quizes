Based on the MEXT English examination papers from 2015 through 2019 provided in the sources, here is the definitive exam pattern.

The exam structure has remained consistent across these years. To generate an accurate mock exam, you must adhere to the **60-minute** time limit for **50 questions**, which allows approximately **1.2 minutes per question**.

### **Exam Pattern & Mock Generator Guide**

#### **Section I: Vocabulary in Context**
*   **Format:** 10 Questions (Multiple Choice A–D).
*   **Task:** Select the word that best completes the sentence.
*   **Hardness:** **Medium-High (B2/C1 Level).**
    *   The challenge lies in **nuance**. The options often look similar or belong to the same category (e.g., medical terms, emotional states) but only one fits the specific context.
*   **Word Count:** Single sentences (15–25 words).
*   **Source Reference:** Past questions asked to distinguish between *diagnoses/symptoms* or *disengaged/disgruntled*.

**Mock Sample Questions:**
1.  Although the director’s speech was long, the audience remained \_\_\_\_\_\_ to the very end because of her charisma.
    *   A) attached
    *   B) attentive
    *   C) attractive
    *   D) available
    *   *(Target Skill: Adjective nuance)*

2.  The scientist \_\_\_\_\_\_ the failure of the experiment to a sudden change in temperature.
    *   A) attributed
    *   B) contributed
    *   C) distributed
    *   D) tribute
    *   *(Target Skill: Verbs with similar roots)*

---

#### **Section II: Grammar & Phrasing**
*   **Format:** 10 Questions (Multiple Choice A–D).
*   **Task:** Select the correct grammatical structure or phrasal verb.
*   **Hardness:** **High.**
    *   Focuses on specific rules such as **conditionals** (if/had), **inversion**, **gerunds vs. infinitives**, and **prepositions**.
*   **Word Count:** Single sentences (15–30 words).
*   **Source Reference:** Questions often test structures like "mind *my lying*" or "If Alice *had known*... she *would have*".

**Mock Sample Questions:**
1.  \_\_\_\_\_\_ for the swift action of the fire department, the building would have burned down.
    *   A) If it is not
    *   B) Had it not been
    *   C) Should it not be
    *   D) Were it not
    *   *(Target Skill: Inverted conditional/Past hypothetical)*

2.  The new software is superior \_\_\_\_\_\_ the old version in terms of processing speed.
    *   A) from
    *   B) than
    *   C) to
    *   D) with
    *   *(Target Skill: Collocations/Prepositions)*

---

#### **Section III: Error Identification**
*   **Format:** 10 Questions.
*   **Task:** A sentence or short paragraph contains 4 underlined sections (labeled A, B, C, D). You must identify the **one** that is incorrect.
*   **Hardness:** **Very High.**
    *   The errors are often subtle, involving **singular/plural agreement**, **verb tense consistency**, or **article usage** hidden within complex sentence structures.
*   **Word Count:** Short paragraphs (40–60 words).
*   **Source Reference:**
    *   *2019 Exam:* "the virus itself did not **caused** all of the deaths" (Error: caused → cause).
    *   *2015 Exam:* "informations regarding..." (Error: information is uncountable).

**Mock Sample Questions:**
1.  (A) *Despite* the heavy rain, the soccer match (B) *continued* as planned, (C) *much* to the delight of the fans who (D) *was* waiting for hours.
    *   *(Answer: D. Should be "were" to match "fans".)*

2.  (A) *According* to recent studies, (B) *sleep* deprivation can lead to serious health issues, (C) *included* heart disease and (D) *high* blood pressure.
    *   *(Answer: C. Should be "including".)*

---

#### **Section IV: Cloze Test (Fill in the Blanks)**
*   **Format:** 1 Long Passage with 10 blanks.
*   **Task:** Select the best word from the list to fill the blanks.
*   **Hardness:** **Medium.**
    *   Focuses on **narrative flow**, **conjunctions** (however, therefore), and **verbs** that fit the tense of the story.
*   **Word Count:** 300–400 words total.
*   **Source Reference:** Past topics include Marcel Proust, Global Population history, and the history of the word "cool".

**Mock Sample Question (Excerpt):**
*Passage Context: A short text about the history of coffee.*
"Coffee was first discovered in Ethiopia. Legend says a goat herder noticed his goats became energetic after eating certain berries. \_\_\_(1)\_\_\_, the drink did not become popular globally until much later. By the 17th century, coffee houses had \_\_\_(2)\_\_\_ centers for social interaction."

1.  *Options:* A) Consequently, B) However, C) Therefore, D) Unless
2.  *Options:* A) become, B) became, C) becoming, D) comes

---

#### **Section V: Reading Comprehension**
*   **Format:** 2 Passages (Part I and Part II), each with 5 questions.
*   **Task:** Read the text and answer multiple-choice questions.
*   **Hardness:** **High.**
    *   Questions require **inference**, **detail retrieval**, and identifying **True/False** statements. The texts are academic or journalistic essays.
*   **Word Count:** 500–700 words per passage.
*   **Source Reference:**
    *   *Topics:* Scientific phenomena (Louis Pasteur and chirality), Sociology (Food waste in the UK), or Linguistics (Snow words).
    *   *Question Style:* "According to the article, which statement is TRUE?".

**Mock Sample Question:**
*Passage Topic: The impact of artificial light on bird migration.*
(Imagine a 500-word text explaining how city lights confuse birds).

1.  Based on the passage, why do migrating birds often circle above cities at night?
    *   A) They are attracted to the warmth of the buildings.
    *   B) They become disoriented by the artificial glow.
    *   C) They are looking for food in trash bins.
    *   D) They are waiting for the sun to rise to navigate.
    *   *(Target Skill: Cause and effect inference).*

2.  Which of the following statements is **NOT** mentioned as a solution in the text?
    *   A) Turning off skyscraper lights during migration season.
    *   B) Using blue-spectrum LED lights in streetlamps.
    *   C) Installing protective nets around glass buildings.
    *   D) Reducing the brightness of billboard advertisements.
    *   *(Target Skill: Detail verification).*
